# Göktürk's Digital Showcase

Lovable.dev platformuna yapıştırabileceğin, yeşil ve beyaz tonlarında, mikro animasyonlu ve yüksek estetiğe sahip profesyonel prompt:

Plaintext

Build a high-end, modern, and aesthetic personal portfolio website for Göktürk Günbatı, a High School Senior specializing in Software Development and Technology Research, targeting a Business Informatics (MIS) major.

### Color Palette & Design Aesthetics
- Color Theme: Minimalist Emerald & Clean White. Deep forest emerald green (#064e3b, #047857), mint green accents (#10b981, #34d399), soft mint/slate light backgrounds (#f0fdf4 or clean #f8fafc), and crisp pure white (#ffffff) cards with dark slate typography (#0f172a).
- Style: Modern Glassmorphism with soft borders, clean card elevation, smooth micro-interactions, subtle gradient blur spheres in the background, and refined typography (Inter or System Sans).

### Layout & Structure
1. Hero Section:
   - Large bold header: "Göktürk Günbatı"
   - Subtitle: "High School Senior | Software Developer & Technology Researcher"
   - Target Major Tag: "Targeting Business Informatics / Management Information Systems (MIS)"
   - Interactive CTA Buttons with hover animation: "LinkedIn (800+ Network)" and "GitHub".

2. Interactive Projects Section ("Tech & Research Projects"):
   - Display 3 distinct modern cards with pill tags at the top:
     a) Tag: "3 Consecutive Years" | Title: "TEKNOFEST Competitions" | Desc: "Led software design, system architecture, and technical documentation for Python-based engineering projects."
     b) Tag: "National Competition" | Title: "TÜBİTAK 2204 Research" | Desc: "Engineered research software and conducted data analysis on environmental effects (Radon gas study) for national evaluation."
     c) Tag: "Harvard CS50P Capstone" | Title: "Digital Health App" | Desc: "Designed and deployed a specialized digital health application featuring algorithmic data processing and database operations."

3. Certifications & Academic Strengths Section:
   - Two balanced multi-line cards:
     a) "Technical Certifications": Harvard CS50P Python Certified, WHO Digital Health Systems Certified, Generative AI & Power BI Training (BTK Akademi), IBM Data Science & AI (Ongoing).
     b) "Languages & Academics": English (TOEFL iBT Candidate - Sep 2026), Russian (A1 Certified - RUDN / Stepik), Academic Honors (GPA 95/100 in 11th Grade).

4. Extracurriculars & Athletics Section:
   - "School Theater Lead & Debate Champions": Lead actor in high school theater productions and 9th grade debate champion.
   - "Regional Athletics": 1st Place Winner in Bursa Regional Weightlifting Championship & Traditional Turkish Archery competitor.

5. Footer:
   - Clean, centered text: "© 2026 Göktürk Günbatı. All rights reserved."

Ensure all cards have subtle zoom/rise on hover, slick border highlights, responsive grid lay
